How CampusFix Works

A transparent 4-step loop connecting students with college administrative departments.

STEP 01
👁️

Spot an Issue

Notice broken equipment, Wi-Fi failure, or water leaks around campus.

STEP 02
📝

Report It

Submit location, priority, description, and optional photo in under a minute.

STEP 03
📍

Track Progress

Receive a unique tracking ID to follow live status updates from facility staff.

STEP 04
✅

Get It Resolved

College departments inspect, fix the issue, and mark it resolved with audit logs.
